Privacy Policy — Eurobond CRM

Euro Panel Products Limited (Eurobond ACP) · Last updated: 9 September 2026

Eurobond CRM is a private, internal application. It is provided only to employees of Euro Panel Products Limited for managing field sales work. Accounts are created by the company administrator; the app is not open to the general public and has no self sign-up.

1. Information we collect

DataWhy we collect it
Name, employee code, mobile number, email, designation, reporting manager, state / zone / depot To identify the employee and apply role-based access
Location (including in the background) To record attendance and the day's field-visit route while the employee is on duty
Photographs taken in the app (attendance selfie, expense bills, visiting cards, site photos) To verify attendance, process expense claims and capture customer details
Customer and enquiry details entered by the employee To run the company's sales process
Device battery level and network status at each location point To explain gaps in a tracking route
Push notification token To deliver work notifications to the employee's device

2. Background location

The app collects location in the background only after the employee starts attendance for the day, and stops collecting when attendance is stopped or automatically at 10:30 PM. A permanent notification is shown on the device the whole time tracking is running, so the employee always knows it is active. Location is never collected on holidays, before attendance is started, or after it is stopped.

3. How the information is used

  • Attendance, field-visit routes and distance reports
  • Enquiries, customers, quotations, projects, targets and expense approvals
  • Work notifications and reminders

We do not use this data for advertising, and we do not sell or rent it.

4. Sharing

Data is visible only to the employee, their reporting manager and company administrators. It is not shared with any third party except the service providers that host and deliver the app:

  • Hostinger — application server and database hosting (India)
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — push notification delivery
  • Google Gemini API — reading a visiting card photo when the employee uses "Scan Card"
  • WhatsApp Business API (via PingMate) — sending a thank-you message to a customer after a visit

5. Storage and retention

Data is stored on secured servers in India. Location points older than 90 days are deleted automatically. Other work records are kept for as long as the employee's account is active and as required by company policy.

6. Security

All communication uses HTTPS. Passwords are stored hashed, access is controlled by role, and configuration and credential files are blocked from public access.

7. Your choices

  • Location, camera and notification permissions can be turned off in Android settings at any time — attendance tracking will not work without them.
  • To see, correct or delete your data, contact your company administrator.
  • When an employee leaves, the administrator deactivates the account and the personal data is removed on request.

8. Children

The app is for company employees only and is not directed at children under 18.

9. Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with a new "last updated" date.
